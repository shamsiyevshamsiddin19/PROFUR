## Review workflow
Draft -> Team review -> Open questions to PM -> Customer clarification -> Approved decision -> Implementation.

The PM owns scope, deadlines and customer coordination. The Team Lead owns documentation consistency, technical planning, integration and review of AI-produced code.

Do not assume code generated by AI meets acceptance criteria without human review and executable tests. Do not silently expand MVP scope.
